# WeevCrafts UI/UX opportunities — 10 October 2026

This is a recommendation report, not an implementation or release approval. Application code and business records were not changed. Local demo sign-in sessions were used to inspect authenticated screens.

## Recommended direction

Keep the accepted ivory, forest-green and terracotta palette, Playfair Display headings and DM Sans interface text. Make the storefront feel like a curated Indian craft exhibition: accurate product photographs, recognizable craft details, specific maker stories and confident buying information. Make the portals feel calm and efficient through clearer work priorities, readable records and consistent actions.

The strongest signature opportunity is a real **maker-to-object story**: portrait, place, technique, process detail and the resulting product. Use verified content; the current generic artisan photograph must not imply it depicts a particular seller.

## Evidence and limits

Live screens inspected during this review: homepage, product listing, Indigo Everyday Tote detail, Weaver's Touch storefront, empty cart, sign-in, signed-in checkout address step and customer account, seller dashboard and new-product form, marketplace dashboard and support dashboard. Desktop observations used 1440 × 1000; narrow observations used 390 × 844. Product detail had no whole-page horizontal overflow at 390px. Support navigation opened with Enter, moved focus into the drawer, advanced with Tab and returned focus to the trigger on Escape.

No order was placed, product saved, support reply sent or status changed. Payment, submission/error recovery, full screen-reader behavior, native zoom, physical devices and all eight role workflows were not retested. Finance, Returns Operations and Super Admin recommendations below use shared source and the existing audit, rather than a new live survey of those roles. The audit browser tab was closed before the final continuation, so no fresh 320px check was completed.

Read the existing `docs/ui-ux-audit-2026-10-09.md` alongside this report. Subsequent header and secure-customer changes already addressed some earlier findings. Do not treat its old scores or all findings as the current baseline.

Codebase-memory reported an index generation of `2026-10-10T05:49:48Z`. Coverage checks returned partial templ coverage and metadata changes; relevant source was read directly. Graph absence was not treated as evidence of missing behavior.

## Motion: small, purposeful additions

| Location | Before | After / suggested motion | Purpose and frequency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shared shopping buttons (`customer-polish.css:21`) | Color/shadow transitions; no explicit press transform in the inspected shared rule. | Pointer press scale 0.98, transform 120ms with cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1). Keep dimensions stable, keyboard activation immediate and reduced-motion behavior aligned with current project rules. | Feedback; repeated but near-imperceptible. |
| Checkout section changes (`pages.templ:933`) | Steps switch with x-show, without a transition on the inspected panels. | Optional pointer-triggered opacity entrance from 0 to 1 over 160ms with the same curve, no movement; update focus to the step heading immediately. Keyboard and reduced-motion users get an immediate switch. | Prevent a jarring change; occasional. Prototype before adopting. |

Rejected: animated operational rows/charts (hinders reading), staggered search results (frequent and delays scanning), elaborate hero parallax/cursor effects (no demonstrated task benefit), and new drawer animation work (existing customer motion and keyboard behavior already have focused verification).

The app needs limited motion. Press feedback is the highest-leverage small addition; imagery, hierarchy and workflow clarity should receive most of the effort. A later `improve-animations plan` can turn an approved row into an implementation plan.
